Family Business: Mistakes, Successes, and Lessons

Bujinlkham Bold Chief Strategy Officer & Co-Founder

You might not run a family business yourself. However, the chances are exceptionally high that you work for one, partner with one, purchase from one, or will work with a family-owned enterprise in the near future.

Do you know how to successfully navigate and thrive within a family business environment? Would you like to gain deeper insight into these unique corporate dynamics?

Be sure to follow our weekly publication series right here on our website.

Family enterprises are far from a niche corner of the global economy—they are its primary driving force.

"According to PwC's 2025 Global Family Business Survey, citing UN estimates, family-owned or family-controlled enterprises generate approximately two-thirds of global GDP and account for roughly 60% of all worldwide employment."

If you are currently seeking opportunities, there is a high probability you will join a family enterprise. If you are already employed, there is an equally strong likelihood that behind your organization lies a family holding a substantial equity stake or leading executive governance.

Yet, there is one profound dimension that fundamentally differentiates family businesses from standard corporations.

BUSINESS + OWNERSHIP + FAMILY

In conventional corporations, executive discourse centers predominantly on standard operational axes: strategy, finance, market capture, execution, and human resources.

In family businesses, two overarching forces are layered directly on top: ownership architecture and family relationships.

Founders may be parents. Children become the next generation of shareholders. Siblings manage distinct divisions. Some family members actively work within operations; others remain strictly equity holders. Meanwhile, non-family professional executives are brought in to manage day-to-day corporate functions.

As the enterprise scales, deceptively simple yet profoundly challenging questions inevitably surface:

  • ❓ Who holds ultimate decision-making authority?
  • ❓ Who will succeed as the next leader?
  • ❓ What professional qualifications must family members meet to join the company?
  • ❓ How will ownership and equity transition smoothly across generations?
  • ❓ How do you maintain a strict boundary between family and corporate finances?
  • ❓ How can family owners and external professional executives collaborate effectively?

When managed effectively, a family enterprise's long-term orientation, profound trust, agile decision-making, deeply held values, and generational wisdom represent unbeatable competitive advantages.

However, when corporate governance, formal rules, and accountabilities remain ambiguous, these exact strengths can quickly invert into the enterprise's greatest vulnerabilities.

"Industry research shows that among every 10 family businesses, only around 3 successfully survive into the 2nd generation, roughly 1 survives into the 3rd generation, and only about 3 out of 100 endure into the 4th generation and beyond."

WHY ARE WE LAUNCHING THIS ARTICLE SERIES?

Khuvsgul AI Co-Founder Bujinlkham Bold dedicated her Executive MBA thesis at the University of Cambridge Judge Business School to the study of family business dynamics. While vast academic research and literature exist on family businesses, the overwhelming majority of documented cases focus on Western conglomerates. As a family business entrepreneur myself, this topic is not merely academic research—it represents essential, daily business intelligence.

Rather than confining these insights to an MBA dissertation, sharing them with entrepreneurs, executives, and managers through a structured series offers actionable value to the broader business ecosystem.
